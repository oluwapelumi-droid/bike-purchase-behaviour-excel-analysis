## Three interview answers to rehearse

**Q: Why use purchase rates instead of counts?**  
A: Customer groups have different sizes. Comparing the proportion who purchased within each group helps avoid confusing a large segment with a high propensity segment. I still show both count and rate because audience volume matters.

**Q: What did you improve in the original workbook?**  
A: I corrected misleading age labels, expanded the analysis beyond three initial charts, introduced KPIs and region filtering, and documented the assumptions and limits of the dataset.

**Q: Can you conclude that short commutes cause bicycle purchases?**  
A: No. This is descriptive observational analysis. Age, location, car ownership, or other factors might explain the differences. I would validate the hypothesis with more current data and a controlled marketing test.
